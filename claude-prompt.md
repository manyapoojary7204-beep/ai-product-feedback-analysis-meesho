# AI Prompt Used for Product Feedback Analysis

## Objective
Analyze customer reviews from the Meesho app and identify recurring user problems, themes, sentiment, and opportunities for product improvement.

## Prompt
You are a product manager conducting user research.
I will provide 20 customer reviews from the Meesho app.

Please:
1. Categorize each review into a clear theme.
2. Identify the main pain point in each review.
3. Classify the sentiment as Positive or Negative.
4. Count the frequency of each theme.
5. Identify the top 3 customer pain points.
6. Suggest a product improvement for each major pain point.
7. Prioritize the improvements as High, Medium, or Low.
8. Suggest measurable success metrics for the recommended improvements.
9. Summarize the positive aspects customers appreciate.

Do not invent information that is not present in the reviews.
## Input

The input dataset contains 20 Meesho app reviews.
## Output

The analysis should include:
- Review-by-review classification
- Theme frequency
- Top customer pain points
- Recommended product improvements
- Priority levels
- Success metrics
- Final product recommendation
